Hi Pritam, details for the capstone below. Full write-up: docs/CAPSTONE_SUMMARY.md in the repo.

AREA OF DEPLOYMENT
- Built for service operations: any operation where work is handed over between people and delays, rework or safety matter
- Target sectors: automotive service, equipment maintenance, facilities management, utilities and energy, healthcare equipment, aviation maintenance
- Showcased in automotive service, where it is built and demonstrated end to end
- Same users in every sector: the people doing the work feed in updates; supervisors look things up and ask questions
- Moving to a new sector means swapping the catalogue, spec limits and lifecycle; the core is generic
- Runs on one NVIDIA Brev L40S with three self-hosted NIMs

BUSINESS PROBLEM
- Job status is scattered across notes, calls, messages and systems
- Challenge: moving it accurately, and finding it again when it matters
- Time lost re-asking, searching and re-typing
- Resources over-committed: crews double-booked, parts ordered twice
- Critical findings buried: delays, rework, safety incidents
- AI answers can't be traced or checked

NV STACK (13 components, all running)
- NIMs: Nemotron Nano 8B, NV-EmbedQA E5, NV-RerankQA 4B
- Riva: Parakeet (speech-to-text), Magpie (text-to-speech)
- NeMo: Guardrails, Curator, Evaluator, Relay, Agent Toolkit, Switchyard
- Infrastructure: DCGM, Brev L40S 48 GB

RESULTS / PROGRESS
- Built, deployed and demoed end to end in automotive service, with a 2:34 client demo video
