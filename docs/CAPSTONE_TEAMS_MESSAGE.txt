Hi Pritam, details for the capstone below. Full write-up: docs/CAPSTONE_SUMMARY.md in the repo.

AREA OF DEPLOYMENT
- Service operations, built and demonstrated for automotive service
- Technicians feed in updates by voice or text; supervisors look things up and ask questions
- Runs on one NVIDIA Brev L40S with three self-hosted NIMs
- Other sectors (equipment, facilities, utilities, aviation) are positioning only; automotive is the one built

BUSINESS PROBLEM
- Job information is spread across notes, calls, messages and systems
- The hard part is moving it accurately and finding it again when it matters
- Time is lost: people ask again, search and re-type the same update
- Resources get over-committed: double-booked jobs, the same part ordered twice
- Safety findings get buried in notes and missed at handover

NV STACK (14 components: 13 running, 1 blocked)
- NIMs: Nemotron Nano 8B, NV-EmbedQA E5, NV-RerankQA 4B
- Riva: Parakeet (speech-to-text), Magpie (text-to-speech)
- NeMo: Guardrails, Curator, Evaluator, Relay, Agent Toolkit, Switchyard
- Infrastructure: DCGM, Brev L40S 48 GB
- Blocked: NemoClaw / OpenShell (Brev platform fault)

RESULTS / PROGRESS
- Built, deployed and demoed end to end; 2:34 client demo video made
- 81 unit tests pass; 14 of 14 live answer checks pass
- Routing, grounding, refusal and traceability all score 100%
- Structured answers in about 0.1 s with no model call; free-text answers in 3 to 4 s locally
- Weakest area: retrieval recall@6 is 83.3%

NEXT STEPS
- Improve retrieval recall
- Fix answer composition in the Agent Toolkit workflow
- Unblock NemoClaw / OpenShell
- Add a local Riva speech-to-text container
- Add authentication and access control
- Connect real sources (work orders, parts, scheduling)
- Pilot with a real service operation
